# OcuSmart for Claude — Privacy notice

Updated: 16 October 2026 · Publisher and scientific director: Alfredo Di Giovanni, MD · Contact: ocusmartai@gmail.com

This notice covers the OcuSmart plugin and skill for Claude and OcuSmart's non-clinical support email. It is not a legal certification and does not claim GDPR or HIPAA compliance.

## 1. What the plugin is
OcuSmart supplies specialist instructions and descriptive calculation code that run inside Claude. It has no server, database, account system, analytics or telemetry of its own, and it does not send case material, conversations or files to the publisher or to any third party.

## 2. How content is processed
Everything a user submits is processed by Claude under the user's own Claude account settings and Anthropic's applicable terms and privacy policy. OcuSmart does not change those settings and cannot access, retain or delete conversations. When literature retrieval is requested, OcuSmart uses Claude's own web search and fetch with general scientific terms or bibliographic metadata only, never case data, files or images. The bundled calculation script runs locally in Claude's code-execution environment, writes no files and makes no network calls. OcuSmart instructs Claude not to save case information to memory.

## 3. Permitted material
Only synthetic cases or thoroughly anonymized real material may be submitted. Removing names is not enough if a person can still be identified from dates, places, rare combinations, images, filenames or metadata. Do not upload originals for OcuSmart to anonymize.

## 4. Precautionary stop
If OcuSmart detects, or cannot exclude, a reasonable risk of identification, it is instructed to stop the analysis and ask for a newly anonymized version. This is an instruction-based safeguard, not a guarantee that every identifier will be detected.

## 5. Support email
Send only minimal, non-clinical information: no patient records, examination images, identifying screenshots or passwords. Support correspondence is used only to handle the request, is not used for marketing or profiling, and is kept for no more than six months after the request is closed. You may ask by email for access to, correction of or deletion of your support correspondence.

## 6. Claude content
For conversations, files and memory in Claude, use the controls and privacy-request channels provided by Anthropic.

OcuSmart is an independent project. It is not affiliated with or endorsed by Anthropic.
