# OcuSmart for Claude — Terms of use

Updated: 16 October 2026 · Publisher and scientific director: Alfredo Di Giovanni, MD · Contact: ocusmartai@gmail.com

## 1. Who it is for
OcuSmart is an advanced research platform in generative artificial intelligence applied to ophthalmology, intended exclusively for ophthalmologists, eye-care and healthcare professionals, researchers and supervised trainees. It is not a patient-facing service.

## 2. Purpose
OcuSmart is experimental and not clinically validated. It must not be used for diagnosis, screening, treatment, surgical planning or any patient-care decision. The experimental label does not create any regulatory exemption.

## 3. Inputs
Remove direct and indirect identifiers, exact dates, identifying filenames and metadata before submitting material. Use relative intervals for longitudinal comparisons. Submit only synthetic or previously anonymized material, for research or education.

## 4. Privacy stops
When a privacy risk is detected, the analysis stops. Identifiers are not repeated, and rejected material must be prepared again outside OcuSmart rather than reprocessed.

## 5. Verification
Outputs may be incomplete, wrong or not reproducible, and platform or model changes may alter behaviour. Users must check sources, calculations and references before relying on them.

## 6. Access and cost
OcuSmart is free and has no purchases, subscriptions or payments of its own. Access to Claude, its limits and any charges are governed separately by the user's Claude plan and Anthropic's terms, together with any applicable institutional requirements.

## 7. Changes
Features may be revised or withdrawn. No uninterrupted availability or accuracy is promised.

## 8. Support
Non-clinical support is available by email (see SUPPORT.md). Do not send clinical attachments or patient information.
